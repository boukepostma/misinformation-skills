# Misinformation skills

Language for the skills in this repo that combat misinformation: the claims they counter, the corrections they produce, and the techniques behind the claims. Distilled from Skeptical Science's approach (see `research/`).

## Language

**Misinformation**:
A false or inaccurate claim, regardless of the speaker's intent.

_Avoid_: fake news, false news

**Disinformation**:
Misinformation spread deliberately to deceive.

_Avoid_: propaganda, misinformation (in the deliberate case)

**Myth**:
A recurring false claim that has been catalogued and rebutted; the unit of work in a topic's myth catalog.

_Avoid_: lie, hoax, fake news

**Rebuttal**:
An evidence-based correction of a myth, structured Fact–Myth–Fallacy: state the fact, name the myth (after warning the reader), expose the technique, and present the full picture.

_Avoid_: debunk, takedown, correction

**Technique**:
The rhetorical or logical device a myth uses to mislead — FLICC: fake experts, logical fallacies, impossible expectations, cherry picking, conspiracy theories. Preferred over "fallacy" because it also covers non-logical devices.

_Avoid_: fallacy, gimmick, trick

**Prebunking**:
Exposing a myth or its technique in advance so people build resistance before they meet it (inoculation).

_Avoid_: inoculation, debunking

**Topic**:
A self-contained domain module under `topics/` with its own overview, myth catalog, and sources (e.g. climate).

_Avoid_: subject, category, domain
