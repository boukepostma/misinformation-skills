# Attribution

The method and the vendored climate myth catalog in this repo are based on [Skeptical Science](https://skepticalscience.com) — founded in 2007 by John Cook, a volunteer-run non-profit publishing evidence-based refutations of climate misinformation.

## Licenses

- skepticalscience.com website content (argument list, one-line rebuttals, claim titles): [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Vendored myth files quote these short phrases with attribution and a link to the originating argument page.
- [The Scientific Guide to Global Warming Skepticism](https://skepticalscience.com/docs/Guide_to_Skepticism.pdf) (John Cook, 2010): [CC BY-NC 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/). Cited and linked only — its text is never copied into this repo.
- The FLICC taxonomy of science-denial techniques (fake experts, logical fallacies, impossible expectations, cherry picking, conspiracy theories) originates from Cook et al. (2015), developed on Skeptical Science. The 34 sub-techniques with definitions and examples in [`docs/techniques/flicc.md`](docs/techniques/flicc.md) are short excerpts from the [FLICC cheat sheet](https://skepticalscience.com/docs/FLICC-Cheat-Sheet.pdf) (Skeptical Science, August 2022), quoted with attribution and a link; the taxonomy is referenced under `technique` in myth files.
- The Fact–Myth–Fallacy debunking structure and the "warn before naming the myth" rule come from Cook's [How to debunk misinformation](https://skepticalscience.com/How-to-debunk-misinformation.html) (CC BY 3.0).
